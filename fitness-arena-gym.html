Fitness Arena is a G-6 fixture for people who want more than a treadmill and a mirror — personal trainers for every age group, physiotherapy and rehab support, and coaches who know your name by week two.

Members come in as beginners recovering from injury, as athletes chasing a specific number, or as parents bringing their kids along. The floor is built to hold all three at once.

What's on the floor

Four core programs. Everything else — diet plans, one-off sessions, kids' coaching — is built around these.

01 — Training

Personal Training

One-on-one coaching for seniors, adults, athletes, and kids. Programs are built around the person, not a template.

02 — Recovery

Physiotherapy & Rehab

On-site physiotherapy for injury recovery, joint pain, and post-surgery conditioning, supervised by certified staff.

03 — Women only

Zumba Classes

Dedicated female-only Zumba sessions — dance-based cardio in a closed, comfortable setting.

04 — Everyday floor

Strength & Cardio

Free weights, machines, and cardio equipment for members training on their own program.
